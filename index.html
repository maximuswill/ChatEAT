What we're building

The proposed application is an AI-powered personal cooking companion designed to improve the user's everyday cooking experience while helping them progressively develop their cooking abilities and achieve their nutritional and lifestyle goals. Rather than functioning as a conventional recipe search engine or rigid meal planner, the application maintains an evolving understanding of the individual user and uses that information to deliver personalized cooking recommendations.

The application has two primary functions: generating personalized weekly cooking plans and providing immediate, individualized recipe recommendations. The weekly planning feature allows users to describe their upcoming schedule, activities, food preferences, and specific needs. The AI then produces a flexible collection of meal options, including meals suited to particular occasions, quick meals for busy days, meal-preparation opportunities, and additional food that supports the user's goals. The individual recipe feature allows users to make a short request based on cravings, available ingredients, time, or other circumstances and receive recommendations informed by their established profile.

Both functions rely on the same adaptive AI system. It considers cooking abilities, food preferences, available equipment and ingredients, nutritional objectives, spending habits, schedule, and previous feedback. It also helps users develop new skills by gradually introducing appropriate recipes and techniques.

The overall objective is to create a cooking experience that becomes increasingly personalized and useful over time. The application should feel like an intelligent cooking coach that understands the user, accommodates real-life changes, and helps them become a more capable and confident cook—not simply a program that produces recipes.
